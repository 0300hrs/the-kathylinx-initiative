About the initiative

Who we are, what drives the work, and the people carrying it out.

Our mission

The Kathylinx Initiative exists to close the small, specific gaps that keep children out of school and communities without the means to look after their own health and environment.

We started with a simple observation: the children who miss the most school aren't missing it because they don't want to learn. They're missing it because of a fee that wasn't paid, a uniform that wore out, or a lack of basic supplies that turns a normal week into one spent at home. Those are solvable problems, and we built the initiative to solve them directly, in partnership with the schools and families who know their communities best.

Every programme we run is funded by donations and carried out with local partners, so the support reaches the people who need it rather than getting lost in overhead. We publish what we do and where the money goes, and we go back to the same communities year after year rather than moving on once the cameras leave.

Programs we run

Three ongoing efforts, each aimed at a specific barrier we've seen firsthand.

Back to School Project

We cover school fees, books, and uniforms for children at risk of falling out of the school system, and work with head teachers to identify who needs support each term.

Pad a Girl Project

Monthly distribution of sanitary products to girls in partner schools, paired with basic menstrual health education, so a normal part of life stops costing anyone a week of class.

Sustainability Awareness Exercises

Practical, hands-on workshops with households and schools on waste separation, reuse, and basic hygiene, run by local facilitators so the habits stick after we leave.
