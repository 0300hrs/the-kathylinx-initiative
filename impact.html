Our impact

A closer look at the three projects we run and what they've achieved so far.

Pad a Girl Project

Keeping girls in the classroom

Girls without access to sanitary products often miss a week of school every month, and many fall behind or drop out entirely. Since launching, we've supplied monthly sanitary packs to girls across our partner schools, alongside basic menstrual health education delivered by trained volunteers.

6partner schools
210+girls supported monthly

Back to School Project

A fair start to every term

This is the project the initiative was built on. We cover school fees, books, and uniforms for children identified by head teachers as at risk of missing the term, so a family's financial situation doesn't decide whether a child gets an education. Project 2.0 expands this to three additional schools this academic year.

12partner schools
500+children supported

Sustainability Awareness Exercises

Habits that outlast the workshop

Working alongside schools and households, we run hands-on sessions on waste separation, reuse, and basic hygiene practices. Sessions are led by local facilitators rather than outside trainers, so the habits are grounded in how each community already lives and are more likely to stick.

18workshops run
4communities reached
