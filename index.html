What we do

The Kathylinx Initiative works directly with schools and communities to remove the barriers that keep children out of the classroom and families without the basics they need to stay healthy and self-sufficient.

Every project we run comes from a gap we've seen on the ground: a child sent home for lack of school fees, a girl missing a week of school every month, a household without the knowledge to manage its own waste. We fund, organise, and follow through on each one with the communities we serve, not for them.
